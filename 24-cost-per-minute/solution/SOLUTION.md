# Solution, Lesson 24

With transcription at 0.016, the per-minute total rises to 0.119 USD. The hardened system's twelve calls now cost 0.6866 USD in total, and its cost per successful booking rises to 0.0618.

The cost advantage survives comfortably: 0.6866 is still well under the as-built system's original 0.761, let alone its repriced figure, because the advantage never came from cheap components; it came from not spending minutes on failing calls. In general the as-built configuration is more exposed to per-minute rises, because it burns more minutes per outcome (mean call 0.52 minutes against the hardened 0.43, with its longest calls being its failures). Any per-minute increase taxes the wasteful system more, which is the economic form of the reliability argument.

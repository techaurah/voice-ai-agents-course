# Exercise, Lesson 24: A pricier recogniser

Suppose the transcription component doubles in price, from 0.008 to 0.016 USD per minute, other rates unchanged. Recompute the hardened configuration's total spend on the twelve calls and its cost per successful booking (edit the rates in memory, not the fixture file).

Then write two sentences: how much of the hardened system's cost advantage over as-built survives the price rise, and which configuration is more exposed to per-minute price rises in general, and why?

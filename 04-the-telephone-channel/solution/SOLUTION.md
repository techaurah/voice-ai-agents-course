# Solution, Lesson 04

The 300 Hz tone measures 0.04 before the chain and 0.0398 after it, a retention on par with the 1 kHz tone's 0.0384.

300 Hz sits right at the stated lower edge of the telephone passband (roughly 300 to 3,400 Hz), so it just makes the cut the channel imposes. It survives for the same reason 1 kHz does: the band limit is a cliff at the edges, not a slope across the whole band. Content below about 300 Hz and above about 3,400 Hz is discarded or aliased; content inside is carried with only the gentle mu-law quantisation noise (37.3 dB SNR on the fixture utterance) to answer for.

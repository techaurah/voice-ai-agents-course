# Exercise, Lesson 04: A tone inside the band

Run a 300 Hz tone (500 ms, 16 kHz) through the same probes the artifact uses: Goertzel power at its own frequency before the chain, and after the chain at the 8 kHz rate.

Compare the result with the 1 kHz and 6 kHz tones from the README and write two sentences: where in the band is 300 Hz relative to the telephone passband's stated edges, and why does it survive almost as well as 1 kHz?

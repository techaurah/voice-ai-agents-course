# Solution, Lesson 20

The fixed 500 ms fault scores 10 of 12 with 0 escapes and 7 cut-offs; the failing calls are again the slow pair, call-02 and call-11.

The extra 200 ms over the 300 ms fault bought nothing on this fixture set: same success count, same failed calls, and one more cut-off event along the way (7 against 6), because 500 ms still sits below the slow speakers' 680 to 940 ms thinking pauses. Partial threshold increases inside the danger band rearrange the damage without reducing it. The slow-speaker problem is not solved by any fixed number in this range, only by making the wait conditional, which is the adaptive policy's whole argument from Lesson 06.

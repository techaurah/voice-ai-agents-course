# Solution, Lesson 27

Under confirmation-off, all three safety-path calls still succeed: call-05 true, call-07 true, call-09 true.

The safety paths are protected by the flow layer, not the hearing layer: the consent gate, the card refusal, and the emergency escalation all live in the control structure evaluated before or regardless of how values are confirmed, exactly as Lesson 21 showed for consent. And the as-built family's perfect score never implied confirmation is a safety feature in the first place: as-built also lacks confirmation, and its safety paths pass for the same structural reason. Crediting a present-or-absent quality layer for a control layer's behaviour is how teams end up "fixing" safety by tuning the recogniser while the actual gate goes untested.

# Exercise, Lesson 25: Kill the clustering

Replace the arrival model with an even spread (callers distributed uniformly through each hour, the way the capacity plan in a sales deck assumes) and re-run the one-agent simulation. Report served, abandoned, mean wait, and p95 wait.

Then write two sentences: how much of the one-agent disaster was the clustering assumption rather than the volume, and what does that imply about the first question to ask when a customer reports "the phones were chaos at nine"?

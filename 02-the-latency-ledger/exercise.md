# Exercise, Lesson 02: A cheaper wait

Rebuild the ledger with the fixed endpointing wait set to 400 ms instead of 800 ms (change the `vad.endpoint` call in the artifact). Report the new endpointing total and the new grand total.

Then write two sentences: how much of the apparent improvement is real responsiveness, and what the ledger cannot tell you about the cost of the shorter wait. Keep the second answer for Lesson 06, where the cost is measured directly.

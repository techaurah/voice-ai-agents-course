# Solution, Lesson 02

With the wait at 400 ms the endpointing total halves to 9,600 ms and the grand total falls to 20,165 ms, a mean of about 840 ms per turn. Nothing else in the ledger moves, because the wait enters the total additively.

Of that improvement, all of it is real responsiveness for callers who had genuinely finished speaking: they are answered 400 ms sooner. What the ledger cannot show is the other population: callers who were only pausing. The ledger prices completed turns only, so a policy that interrupts slow speakers looks free in this view. Lesson 06 measures the interruption side directly, and on the fixture set the 400 ms policy cuts off all three slow utterances, which is the bill that arrives for this saving.

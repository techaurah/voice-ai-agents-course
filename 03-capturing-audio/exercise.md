# Exercise, Lesson 03: Two quick prices

Using the functions already in `vox/audio.py`:

1. Compute the bytes per minute of 16-bit mono audio at 24 kHz, a rate some recorders default to.
2. Count how many frames the lesson's utterance produces at a 10 ms frame size instead of 20 ms.

Then write one sentence on what the 10 ms frame count does to any per-frame processing loop, and one sentence on whether 24 kHz capture buys a telephone caller anything.

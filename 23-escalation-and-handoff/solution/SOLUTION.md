# Solution, Lesson 23

At a repair cap of 3, call-11 escalates, having used 3 repairs over 9 agent turns. The boundary is therefore between 3 and 4: the call needs exactly 4 repairs to complete, so caps of 2 and 3 abandon it one step short, and caps of 4 and above let it finish.

Knowing the exact boundary matters because a production cap is set for a population, not a call. If most of your hard calls need one repair more than the cap allows, a small cap increase rescues a whole class; if the neediest calls need twice the cap, raising it only prolongs their struggle before the same escalation. The sweep's job is to show the distribution of needs, and this fixture's hardest call needing exactly 4, no more, is what makes cap 4 the defensible setting here rather than a round number.
